Skills · plugins · themes for Claude

Small minds that know one job.

Familiars are Claude skills and plugins built in a Berlin studio for people who make and deliver sound. Each one has a form, a name and a narrow job. It works inside your rules, says what it does not know, and stops when the job is done.

What a familiar is

A narrow helper with house rules built in.

A familiar loads when your request matches its job, and only then. Three rules hold for every one of them.

Narrow

One job, stated exclusions

Each familiar names what it covers and which neighbour to call for the rest, so two of them never answer the same question twice.

Bound

Your specs, never invented ones

No sample rate, loudness target, true-peak ceiling or file format appears unless you supplied it. Missing values come back as a labelled default or a single question.

Honest

Facts kept apart from guesses

What you said, what was measured and what was inferred stay labelled. A familiar never claims it heard, sent or published something it did not.

Catalogue

Seven familiars, each with its sigil.

The sigil is the familiar's mark at small size: one colour, readable at 16 pixels. Hover a card to see it drawn.
