# Mix Revision & Mastering Handoff Kit - product brief

## Buyer problem

Independent artists and engineers routinely lose time to scattered revision notes, ambiguous filenames,
unclear approvals, missing alternate versions and incomplete mastering briefs. The product must provide a
repeatable handoff system without requiring a particular DAW, cloud platform or subscription.

## Commercial position

- Identity: Gabriel Garcia Alonso / Hologram People
- Buyer: independent artists, producers, mix engineers and small labels
- Product: eleven editable workflow templates plus one concise operating guide
- Format: Markdown, CSV, TXT and PDF
- Dependencies: none
- Store: Gumroad first
- Target price: €14

## Boundaries

- Not a legal contract, accounting system or replacement for written client terms.
- No universal loudness, true-peak, revision-count or delivery-format prescriptions.
- Every technical target remains a field to confirm from the actual destination brief.
- No invented platform specifications or promises of fewer revisions.

## Delivery gates

1. Create eleven distinct editable templates.
2. Validate CSV column consistency and required headings.
3. Create and visually inspect the operating guide PDF.
4. Package README, licence, manifest, templates and guide.
5. Seal the ZIP, reopen it and revalidate all customer files.
6. Publish only after Gabriel approves the product utility and storefront presentation.
