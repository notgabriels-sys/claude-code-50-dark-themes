HOW TO USE

Impacts are designed for structural events: drops, hard edits and the end of a phrase. Risers and
downlifters are eight seconds long, which equals four bars at 120 BPM; warp or stretch them to your
session grid. The drones are key-labelled and work as low harmonic pressure beneath an arrangement.
Noise sweeps are intentionally neutral and can be filtered, gated or resampled without fighting an
existing key.

The files retain headroom. They are not mastered to a common loudness because an impact, a drone and
a sustained noise sweep serve different functions. Gain-stage them in context.
