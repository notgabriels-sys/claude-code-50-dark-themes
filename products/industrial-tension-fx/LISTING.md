## Full description

Pressure without the festival vocabulary.

Created by Hologram People, Industrial Tension & Transition FX is a set of 48 transition tools built
for industrial, hypnotic and emotional techno. The pack moves through sub-frequency drops, tuned
metallic decay, rising noise, descending pressure and low harmonic drones. The sounds are direct enough
to place quickly, but retain enough headroom to distort, filter, stretch and resample into your own system.

Everything was synthesized from noise, resonators, oscillation and modulation. There are no recycled
source recordings, construction-kit loops or plugin dependencies.

### Inside

- 10 industrial impacts with sub movement and metallic tails
- 10 eight-second tension risers
- 10 eight-second downlifters
- 8 twelve-second pitched drones: C1, D1, Eb1, F1, Gb1, G1, A1 and Bb1
- 10 four-second noise rises and falls

### Technical

- 48 kHz / 24-bit stereo WAV
- Every file individually checked below −1 dBTP
- Boundary fades and complete tails
- Key labels on pitched material
- Any DAW; no plugins required
- Royalty-free for commercial music, live performance, film, games and client work

The pack is not normalised to one loudness. An impact, a drone and a sustained noise sweep do different
jobs; their dynamics are preserved so they can be gain-staged in the arrangement rather than fighting it.
