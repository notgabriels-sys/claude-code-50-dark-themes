## Full description

Low end is not one sound. It is the relationship between weight, decay, saturation and movement.

Techno Low-End Architecture contains forty-eight original sounds divided into four precise families: centred sub impulses, harmonically dense bass stabs, decaying rumble tails and modulated low-frequency textures. Every family runs chromatically from C1 through B1, so the tonal centre is explicit before you begin tuning or resampling.

Every sound was synthesized from scratch through deterministic oscillation, filtering, noise, modulation and controlled dynamics. There are no recycled source recordings, construction-kit loops or plugin dependencies.
