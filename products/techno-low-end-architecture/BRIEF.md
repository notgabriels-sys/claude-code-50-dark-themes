# Techno Low-End Architecture — product brief

## Commercial position

- Identity: Hologram People
- Product: 48 original low-end one-shots and tails
- Buyer: intermediate and professional techno producers
- Sound: industrial, hypnotic, controlled, mono-safe below the stereo detail layer, and suitable for processing
- Format: DAW-agnostic stereo WAV, 24-bit / 48 kHz
- Dependencies: none
- Store: Gumroad first, Bandcamp-ready archive
- Proposed price: €17

## Content contract

- `01_Sub_Pulses`: 12 chromatically tuned, centred sub impulses from C1 through B1
- `02_Saturated_Bass_Stabs`: 12 chromatically tuned, harmonically dense bass one-shots
- `03_Rumble_Tails`: 12 chromatically anchored, decaying industrial low-end tails
- `04_Low_Frequency_Motion`: 12 chromatically anchored, modulated low-frequency textures
- Total: exactly 48 WAV files

## Design constraints

- No complete kick drums, percussion, risers, impacts or general-purpose drones; this pack must remain distinct from the existing catalog.
- Every sound must be synthesized from scratch through deterministic oscillation, filtering, noise, modulation and dynamics.
- Tonal filenames must state the actual root note without fabricated hardware or recording claims.
- Sub pulses must remain sample-identical between left and right channels. Other families may carry restrained stereo information only above the low-frequency foundation.
- Preserve processing headroom and category-specific dynamics rather than forcing every file to one common loudness.
- Use zero-valued beginning and ending boundaries, complete decay or designed fade tails, and no full-scale samples.

## Delivery gates

1. Render all 48 files and a representative audition reel.
2. Verify exact count, category counts, filenames, manifest, codec, sample rate, bit depth, channels, duration and nonzero size.
3. Measure boundaries, clipping, DC offset, RMS, sample peak, true peak, stereo correlation and low-band side-to-mid energy for every file.
4. Package README, licence and manifest with the WAVs.
5. Seal the final ZIP, reopen it and rerun the complete technical QA against the extracted customer archive.
6. Create and inspect 1280 × 720 landscape artwork and an 800 × 800 square thumbnail.
7. Obtain checksum-bound human listening approval for the exact reel and archive.
8. Publish only after explicit action-time approval, seller-side attachment read-back and public checkout verification.
