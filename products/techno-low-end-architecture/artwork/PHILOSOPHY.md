# Pressure Section

Pressure Section treats low frequency as structure rather than spectacle. Twelve vertical sections
represent the chromatic root set, while four horizontal traces refer to the four sound families. The
geometry should feel like an engineering drawing for pressure moving through a sealed chamber: exact,
restrained and physical without borrowing the interface of a synthesizer or mixing tool.

The visual hierarchy uses a carbon ground, mineral-white typography and one cold cobalt signal colour.
The accent is concentrated in the lowest third of the field, where mass and movement are implied by
thickening lines and controlled displacement. Fine graphite rules carry the measurement system into the
background but remain subordinate at storefront scale.

Typography is direct and editorial. The title is large enough to read at thumbnail size, while artist,
file count, family names and format behave as technical annotation. Spacing and alignment should feel
deliberately resolved rather than decorative.

The finished object should sit naturally beside the existing Hologram People audio products while
remaining recognisable through its blue pressure trace and low, architectural composition.
