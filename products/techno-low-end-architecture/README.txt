TECHNO LOW-END ARCHITECTURE
by Hologram People
---------------------------

Forty-eight original low-end sounds for industrial, hypnotic and raw techno. The four families cover
clean sub impulses, saturated bass stabs, decaying rumble tails and modulated low-frequency movement.

CONTENTS

01_Sub_Pulses                 12 centred sub impulses, chromatic C1 through B1
02_Saturated_Bass_Stabs       12 harmonically dense bass one-shots, chromatic C1 through B1
03_Rumble_Tails               12 decaying industrial tails, chromatic C1 through B1
04_Low_Frequency_Motion       12 modulated low-end textures, chromatic C1 through B1

TECHNICAL

Files:           48 WAV files
Format:          WAV PCM, stereo
Sample rate:     48 kHz
Bit depth:       24-bit
Dependencies:    none
DAW:             any DAW that reads WAV files

The sub-pulse family is fully centred. The other families keep their low-frequency foundation centred
while restrained stereo detail appears higher in the spectrum. The files retain processing headroom
and are not normalised to one common loudness.

Use the files directly, tune them further in your sampler, shorten them with envelopes, or reshape
them through saturation, filtering, resampling and spatial processing. When layering with a kick,
check polarity, phase and total low-frequency level in the context of the track.
