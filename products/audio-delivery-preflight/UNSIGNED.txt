DEVELOPER-ID-UNSIGNED MACOS BUILD — READ BEFORE OPENING

The application and CLI carry only ad-hoc signatures. An ad-hoc signature
provides local code-integrity structure but no verified publisher identity.
This archive has not been signed with an Apple Developer ID certificate and
has not been notarized by Apple. Apple therefore has not checked this build for
malicious software, and macOS Gatekeeper may block or warn about the app.
This is not a normal one-click installation.

Before opening the app:

1. Download both the ZIP and its `.sha256` file from the seller.
2. In Terminal, run `shasum -a 256 -c <checksum-file>` and continue only if it
   reports `OK` for the ZIP.
3. Move the app to Applications and try to open it once.
4. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, continue only if you trust
   the source and the checksum matched. Open System Settings > Privacy &
   Security, scroll to Security, and use Open Anyway. macOS may require your
   login password. Apple documents this temporary exception at
   https://support.apple.com/102445.

Do not override a warning that the app "will damage your computer" or that the
app is damaged. Delete that copy and obtain a fresh download or contact the
seller through the Gumroad receipt.

a normal one-click installation.
