# Seismic Index

Seismic Index treats pressure as a measurable visual event. Space is divided into dense zones and
quiet margins, as though each composition records impact, decay and residual movement. Forms remain
elementary—bars, thresholds, traces and interrupted fields—but their exact placement implies forces
larger than the page. The balance must feel meticulously calibrated by a master practitioner.

Colour behaves like thermal evidence. Carbon black and mineral grey establish the ground; restrained
ivory identifies structure; one heated orange-red marks energy crossing a boundary. Chromatic intensity
is never ornamental. Every shift should look painstakingly tuned through countless comparisons.

Rhythm emerges through repeated vertical accumulations and abrupt gaps. Dense marks suggest sustained
low-frequency motion, while clean voids act as rests. Scale changes communicate hierarchy without
explanation: one monumental mass, several clinical references, and minute details that reward close
inspection. The result must carry the authority of deep expertise.

Composition is asymmetrical but rigorously weighted. Typography is sparse, integrated as measurement
rather than headline decoration, and given enough air to remain perfectly legible. Alignment, margins
and optical balance receive painstaking attention; no element may appear accidental or mechanically
dropped into place.

The finished artifact should resemble an imaginary scientific plate documenting underground pressure:
emotion translated into exact visual evidence. Its craft must feel laboured over for countless hours,
with every edge, interval and tonal transition resolved at museum-level precision.
