## Full description

One kick does not solve every part of an arrangement.

Raw Techno Kick Architecture separates forty original kick drums into four practical functions: deep
foundations, concise punch, metallic industrial weight and extended rumble tails. Each family is built
to occupy a different role rather than presenting forty near-identical variations of one master.

The kicks were synthesized from tuned oscillation, controlled pitch envelopes, noise, saturation and
generated delay structures. There are no recycled source recordings, construction-kit loops or plugin
dependencies.
