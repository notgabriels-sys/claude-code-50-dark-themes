RAW TECHNO KICK ARCHITECTURE
by Hologram People
---------------------------

Forty original kick drums for industrial, hypnotic and raw techno. The pack separates four useful
functions rather than presenting minor variations of one master: deep foundations, short punch,
metallic industrial impact and extended rumble tails.

CONTENTS

01_Deep          10 low, restrained foundational kicks
02_Punch         10 shorter kicks with a defined transient
03_Industrial    10 saturated kicks with metallic texture
04_Rumble        10 extended kicks with generated rumble tails
