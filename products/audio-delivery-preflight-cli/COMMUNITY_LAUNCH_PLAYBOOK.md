# Audio Delivery Preflight CLI community launch playbook

Last updated: 2026-08-24

Goal: get useful feedback, credibility and sales without annoying the very
engineers, producers, developers and community admins whose trust matters.

## Community-safe principle

Lead with the problem and the sample report, not only the Gumroad link.

Good launch framing:

> I built a small local CLI for checking bounced WAV delivery folders before
> client or label handoff. I would appreciate feedback on whether the sample
> report catches the right boring-but-real delivery issues.

Unsafe launch framing:

> New mastering/audio tool, buy now, please support/upvote.

## Channel decisions

### Do first

1. Product Hunt
   - Fit: good for maker/dev discovery and feedback.
   - Use the clean product URL and square thumbnail.
   - Ask people to visit/comment, not upvote.
   - Requires Gabriel's personal Product Hunt account.

2. KVR Developer Access
   - Fit: strong because KVR explicitly supports product listings for plugins,
     standalone apps, soundware and marketplace/news exposure.
   - Requires KVR account/developer application.

3. AlternativeTo
   - Fit: useful long-tail discovery, but review-gated and slow.
   - Use the product page as the official website.
   - Do not use the user profile as an advertisement.

### Do later / only with care

4. Hacker News
   - Show HN is still not ideal because the paid CLI has no free executable
     trial. If posting to HN, use a normal submission or a feedback-oriented
     technical write-up, not "Show HN", unless a free runnable demo exists.

5. Reddit audio communities
   - High risk for direct self-promo.
   - Do not post a sales link into broad audioengineering/musicproduction
     communities unless a moderator-approved weekly self-promo or feedback
     thread exists.
   - If allowed, share the sample report and ask for critique of the checks.

6. Direct outreach
   - Only one person/org at a time.
   - No attachments.
   - Mention a specific reason the tool may fit their workflow.
   - Show Gabriel the exact recipient and message before sending.

## Red lines

- Do not ask for upvotes.
- Do not incentivize likes, comments, reviews or votes.
- Do not post the same text across many communities.
- Do not claim it replaces mastering, QC listening or distributor checks.
- Do not imply sales, purchases or customer delivery unless verified.
- Do not create paid ads or boosted posts without Gabriel's explicit approval.
- Do not send DMs or emails without exact recipient/message approval.
