# Audio Delivery Preflight CLI community launch playbook

Last updated: 2026-08-24

Goal: get useful feedback, credibility and sales without annoying the very
engineers, producers, developers and community admins whose trust matters.

## Community-safe principle

Lead with the problem, the free checklist and the sample report, not only the
Gumroad link.

Good launch framing:

> I made a free WAV delivery handoff checklist and a small local CLI for
> checking bounced WAV delivery folders before client or label handoff. I would
> appreciate feedback on whether the checklist and sample report catch the right
> boring-but-real delivery issues.

Unsafe launch framing:

> New mastering/audio tool, buy now, please support/upvote.

## Channel decisions

### Do first

1. Product Hunt
   - Fit: good for maker/dev discovery and feedback.
   - Use the clean product URL and square thumbnail.
   - Use the press kit gallery images in order if a gallery is available.
   - Ask people to visit/comment, not upvote.
   - Requires Gabriel's personal Product Hunt account.
   - Current gate: the account welcome email arrived on 2026-08-23. Product
     Hunt's current help center says new accounts must complete onboarding
     before posting, so use the signed-in dashboard as the gate and prepare
     normally rather than forcing an early launch.

2. KVR Developer Access
   - Fit: strong because KVR explicitly supports product listings for plugins,
     standalone apps, soundware and marketplace/news exposure.
   - Requires KVR account/developer application.

3. AlternativeTo
   - Fit: useful long-tail discovery, but review-gated and slow.
   - Use the product page as the official website.
   - Do not use the user profile as an advertisement.

### Do later / only with care

4. Hacker News
   - Do not retry. The account currently has a `showlim` restriction, and a
     second account or disguised repost would be an improper bypass.

5. Reddit audio communities
   - Do not use Reddit. Gabriel has explicitly excluded every Reddit account,
     current or future, from this campaign.

6. Direct outreach
   - Only one person/org at a time.
   - No attachments.
   - Mention a specific reason the tool may fit their workflow.
   - Show Gabriel the exact recipient and message before sending.

## Product Hunt draft

Product name:

> Audio Delivery Preflight CLI

Tagline:

> Check bounced WAV folders before client or label handoff.

Description:

> A local command-line checker for audio delivery folders: WAV evidence,
> delivery hygiene, report output and checksum workflow. Built for producers,
> engineers and labels who want fewer boring handoff mistakes.

First comment:

> I built this from a recurring studio problem: delivery folders often fail on
> boring details before anyone even gets to the listening judgement. Audio
> Delivery Preflight CLI is a local, read-only checker for bounced WAV handoffs.
> It does not master, upload, rename or judge the music. It checks the technical
> package so the human can focus on the actual work.
>
> Sample report:
> https://gabs-utilities.com/audio-delivery-preflight-sample-report.html

## Red lines

- Do not ask for upvotes.
- Do not incentivize likes, comments, reviews or votes.
- Do not post the same text across many communities.
- Do not claim it replaces mastering, QC listening or distributor checks.
- Do not imply sales, purchases or customer delivery unless verified.
- Do not create paid ads or boosted posts without Gabriel's explicit approval.
- Do not send DMs or emails without exact recipient/message approval.
