## Core positioning

One-line:

> Check bounced WAV delivery folders before client, label or release handoff.

Short description:

> Audio Delivery Preflight CLI is a local command-line checker for bounced WAV
> delivery folders. It helps producers, engineers and label operators catch
> mechanical handoff problems before files leave the studio.

Long description:

> Audio Delivery Preflight CLI is a local, read-only preflight checker for
> bounced WAV delivery folders. It is built for producers, mixing engineers,
> mastering engineers, labels and technical collaborators who want one last
> mechanical check before sending a package to a client, label, distributor or
> release workflow. It checks technical WAV evidence, delivery hygiene and
> report/checksum workflow. It does not upload audio, change source files,
> master music, judge artistic quality or replace professional listening.
