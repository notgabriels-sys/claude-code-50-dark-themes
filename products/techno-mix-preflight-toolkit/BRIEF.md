# Techno Mix Preflight Toolkit - product brief

## Buyer problem

Techno producers often reach the end of a mix without a repeatable way to check monitoring level,
low-end audibility, left/right routing, mono cancellation, polarity, frequency emphasis and export
readiness. The product must make those checks fast in any DAW without requiring a plugin or a specific
session format.

## Commercial position

- Identity: Hologram People / Gabriel Garcia Alonso
- Product: calibrated audio test files plus a concise pre-master workflow guide
- Buyer: intermediate producers finishing techno, electro and other low-frequency-focused club music
- Outcome: identify translation and delivery risks before paying for mastering or testing a club system
- Format: DAW-agnostic stereo WAV, 24-bit / 48 kHz, plus PDF and text checklist
- Dependencies: none
- Store: Gumroad first
- Target price: €12

## Content contract

- `01_Level_and_Meter`: 4 conservative level and meter references
- `02_Low_End_Audibility`: 10 low-frequency sine references from 30 Hz to 100 Hz
- `03_Stereo_Mono_and_Polarity`: 6 routing, width and cancellation checks
- `04_Frequency_Focus`: 4 band-limited noise references
- Total: exactly 24 WAV files
- One concise workflow guide PDF
- One printable plain-text checklist
- README, licence and machine-readable manifest

## Safety and claims

- Test files are diagnostic references, not a monitor-calibration certification system.
- All playback instructions must begin at low monitor or headphone volume.
- Do not imply room correction, hearing diagnosis, streaming compliance or guaranteed club translation.
- Document the intended signal level and behavior of every file in the manifest.
- Intentionally out-of-phase files must be clearly named and documented so they cannot be mistaken for
  damaged media.

## Delivery gates

1. Deterministically render all 24 files.
2. Verify count, category counts, codec, sample rate, bit depth, channels, duration and boundaries.
3. Verify expected RMS or intentional channel behavior against the manifest.
4. Render and visually inspect every PDF page.
5. Package all customer materials, seal the ZIP and reopen it.
6. Rerun technical verification from the extracted customer archive.
7. Complete a practical usage pass in a DAW before publication.
8. Publish only after seller-side attachment read-back and public checkout verification.
