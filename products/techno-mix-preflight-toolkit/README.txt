TECHNO MIX PREFLIGHT TOOLKIT
by Hologram People / Gabriel Garcia Alonso
------------------------------------------

A compact set of calibrated audio references and a repeatable pre-master workflow for techno and
other low-frequency-focused club music.

START HERE

1. Turn your monitoring level down.
2. Read Techno_Mix_Preflight_Guide.pdf.
3. Import the WAV files onto clean audio tracks with warping, normalisation, fades and effects off.
4. Do not route the intentional polarity-cancellation file through speakers at high level.
5. Use observations as evidence for a mix decision; do not compensate for every room limitation.

CONTENTS

01_Level_and_Meter             4 conservative meter and level references
02_Low_End_Audibility         10 sine references from 30 Hz to 100 Hz
03_Stereo_Mono_and_Polarity    6 channel, centre, width and cancellation checks
04_Frequency_Focus             4 band-limited noise references

TECHNICAL

Audio files:     24 WAV files
Format:          WAV PCM, stereo
Sample rate:     48 kHz
Bit depth:       24-bit
Dependencies:    none

The toolkit is diagnostic, not corrective. It does not replace acoustic measurement, room treatment,
hearing care, calibrated monitoring or a mastering engineer.
