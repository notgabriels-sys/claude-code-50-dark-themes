HYPNOTIC PERCUSSION GRID
by Hologram People
-----------------------

Sixty-four original percussion one-shots for hypnotic, industrial and raw techno. Four deliberately
separate families cover closed hats, metallic strikes, dry micro-percussion and short noise movement.

CONTENTS

01_Closed_Hats             16 short high-frequency articulations
02_Metallic_Percussion     16 tuned and inharmonic metallic strikes
03_Dry_Clicks_and_Ticks    16 dry micro-percussion sounds
04_Shakers_and_Noise       16 textured shaker and noise articulations

TECHNICAL

Files:           64 WAV files
Format:          WAV PCM, stereo
Sample rate:     48 kHz
Bit depth:       24-bit
Dependencies:    none
DAW:             any DAW that reads WAV files

The sounds retain processing headroom and are not normalised to one common loudness. Sequence them
directly, layer them, or reshape them with envelopes, filtering, distortion and spatial processing.
