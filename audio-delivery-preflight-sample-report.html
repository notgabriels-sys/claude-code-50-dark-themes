What this sample proves, and what it does not

This sample demonstrates report shape and product intent. It is not a guarantee that a distributor, label or streaming service will accept a package. It is not mastering advice and it is not a replacement for listening. It is a practical preflight layer for the boring delivery mistakes that are easy to miss when everyone is focused on the music.
